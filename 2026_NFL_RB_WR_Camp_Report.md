## Buffalo Bills

Offense run by HC/OC Joe Brady with Josh Allen. The 2026 skill-position story is addition-by-perimeter-upgrade (DJ Moore) around a league-best rushing attack.

### James Cook — RB1 (bell-cow / featured back)
1. **Role:** Unquestioned lead back. Signed a four-year, ~$46M extension after a monster 2025.
2. **Camp/preseason:** No competition for his job; the Bills are managing his workload in camp, not auditing it (Yahoo RB preview, July 25, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Coming off leading the entire NFL in rushing (1,621 yards) and rushing TDs (12) in 2025 and earning second-team All-Pro, per Yahoo (July 25, 2026). The organization paid him like a foundational piece.
4. **Competition:** None. Ray Davis and Ty Johnson are complementary.
5. **Injury:** None reported as of early August 2026.
6. **Projected usage:** Every-down workhorse in a run-heavy Brady scheme; goal-line and passing-down work both his. Slight regression off a career year is the base case, but volume + Allen's offense keeps him elite.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** locked-in top-8, arguably top-5 RB; late-1st draft capital. **Dynasty:** freshly paid and only 26, but monitor the classic post-1,600-yard workload cliff — sell-high candidate if you have a contender need elsewhere.

### Ray Davis — RB2 / change-of-pace
1. **Role:** Primary backup / handcuff to Cook.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Enters camp trying to reclaim touches after his production was "cut in half" from his rookie year (58 carries, 275 yards; 2 TD in 2025), per Yahoo (July 25, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Yahoo (July 25, 2026) notes Davis "expressed concerns about workload reduction" and could earn more touches from Joe Brady with a strong camp.
4. **Competition:** Battling Ty Johnson for the No. 2 role and passing-down snaps.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Rotational early-down back; the clear next-man-up if Cook misses time.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** premium handcuff — draftable in the double-digit rounds specifically because Cook's standalone value makes this handcuff league-winning if pressed into duty. **Dynasty:** low-cost stash with real contingent value.

### Ty Johnson — passing-down specialist
1. **Role:** Third-down / passing-down back.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Established, defined role; not a camp battle participant beyond holding his niche.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Valued for pass-pro and receiving (24 catches, 263 yards, 2 receiving TD in 2025 plus 3 rushing TD), per Yahoo (July 25, 2026).
4. **Competition:** Overlaps with Davis on passing downs.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Situational; modest touches.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** deep-league/handcuff-of-a-handcuff only. **Dynasty:** irrelevant; roster depth.

*(Also on roster: Frank Gore Jr., Ian Wheeler — camp bodies; FBs Ben VanSumeren and UDFA Jackson Acker are competing to replace departed Reggie Gilliam — no fantasy relevance.)*

### DJ Moore — WR1 (perimeter alpha)
1. **Role:** New No. 1 perimeter receiver, acquired via trade from Chicago (Bills officially announced the deal; picks swapped in the 2026 draft).
2. **Camp/preseason:** Described as delivering a "spectacular" camp; Josh Allen has leaned on him early (per SI Bills camp report, Aug 5, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Framed as the answer to Buffalo's post-Diggs perimeter void; a "fresh start" after down 2025 numbers in Chicago's chaos (Yahoo WR preview, July 29, 2026). Note: one SI piece ("harsh re-grade") flags the acquisition cost, so the fanbase expectation is high.
4. **Competition:** None for the top job; he's the alpha.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Team-leading targets, boundary + intermediate work with Allen; touchdown upside in a high-scoring offense.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** back-end WR1/high-end WR2 with a real ceiling given the QB upgrade to Allen; 4th–6th round value. **Dynasty:** age (28) caps long-term price, but a strong 2026 in Buffalo re-establishes him.

### Khalil Shakir — WR (slot), the reliable one
1. **Role:** Starting slot; Buffalo's most dependable receiver the last two seasons; role unchanged (Yahoo, July 29, 2026).
2. **Camp/preseason:** Steady; not in any battle.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Trusted chain-mover for Allen; the "known quantity" of the room.
4. **Competition:** None — his slot role is secure.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** High catch rate, PPR-friendly volume; the DJ Moore addition could cap his target ceiling but also clean up his looks.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** steady WR3/flex, best in PPR; 7th–9th round. **Dynasty:** boring but useful; buy the floor.

### Joshua Palmer — WR3 (perimeter)
1. **Role:** Currently penciled as the No. 3 / second outside receiver.
2. **Camp/preseason:** As of Aug 5, 2026 (SI), Palmer leads Keon Coleman for the third receiver spot "but Coleman is closing in, and fast."
3. **Internal thoughts:** Yahoo (July 29, 2026) notes a disappointing first Bills year (22 catches, 303 yards, 0 TD) and that he's "seeking improvement through system familiarity and health."
4. **Competition:** In a live battle with Keon Coleman for the WR3 outside role.
5. **Injury:** None reported currently.
6. **Projected usage:** Rotational perimeter snaps; target share depends on beating Coleman and on DJ Moore/Shakir dominance.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** bench/late flier; only matters if he wins the job and someone ahead misses time. **Dynasty:** minimal.

### Keon Coleman — WR (rising, "make-or-break" year)
1. **Role:** Fighting for the WR3 outside job; entered camp behind Palmer but climbing.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Genuine riser. Per SI (Aug 5, 2026): five days into camp he went "4/4" in one-on-ones vs. CB Max Hairston (Aug 4), was targeted repeatedly by Allen in 7-on-7, and scored a TD in Monday's padded practice — "closing in on his competition, and fast."

4. **Competition:** Directly with Palmer for WR3; rookie Skyler Bell is behind both.
5. **Injury:** History of injuries/inconsistency (context), but healthy and practicing now.
6. **Projected usage:** Boundary "X"/contested-catch role; red-zone size target. Ceiling is a clear WR3 with spike weeks if he wins the job outright.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** best late-round upside dart in this WR room — cheap, ascending, in a great offense. **Dynasty:** buy-low window may be closing; the camp buzz makes him a strong post-hype hold for a young dynasty roster.

### Skyler Bell — rookie WR (2026 draft, Day 3)
1. **Role:** Developmental; competing to be the first receiver off the bench behind the top trio.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Yahoo (July 29, 2026) frames him as an "intriguing prospect with deep-field capability" trying to unseat Coleman; SI (Aug 5) has him "lagging behind" the veterans so far.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Deep-threat traits, but raw; behind on the depth chart in early August.
4. **Competition:** Coleman/Palmer are ahead; he's fighting Hardman/Sherfield types for a roster spot and gadget snaps.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Minimal offensive role expected in 2026; special-teams/rotational.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** none. **Dynasty:** cheap late-round rookie stash on speed/upside only.

*(Depth/special teams: Mecole Hardman and Trent Sherfield battling for final spots; Tyrell Shavers expected to miss significant time per Yahoo, July 29, 2026 — no fantasy relevance.)*

## Miami Dolphins

**The most changed room in the division.** New GM Jon-Eric Sullivan, HC Jeff Hafley, and QB Malik Willis. Achane is the offense; the WR corps is a genuine question mark.

### De'Von Achane — RB1 (workhorse, offensive centerpiece)
1. **Role:** Featured, every-down back and — per SI (July 2026) — "the most indispensable player on offense." Signed a contract extension this offseason.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Tasked with carrying an even larger load with Hill and Waddle gone; team is building the offense around him (SI RB preview; Miami Dolphins RB preview 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Team-MVP-caliber talent; the coaching staff's clear priority is feeding him. Coming off 1,350 rush yards (5.7 avg), 8 rush TD, plus 67 catches / 488 yards / 4 receiving TD in 2025.
4. **Competition:** None for the lead role.
5. **Injury:** No current 2026 injury reported as of early August. (Note: a widely surfaced Achane "calf" story is from 2025 camp — stale; do not apply to 2026.)
6. **Projected usage:** Massive. With a run-leaning, QB-Willis offense, Achane projects for elite touch volume as both runner and the de facto top receiving option out of the backfield. Passing-game funnels through him.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** top-5, plausibly top-3 overall pick — the receiving floor plus rushing ceiling in a Hill/Waddle-less offense is enormous. **Dynasty:** premier asset; only concern is the small frame and workload, but he's the cornerstone.

### Ollie Gordon II — RB2 (power complement)
1. **Role:** Projected No. 2 back / early-down and short-yardage complement.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Competing with Jaylen Wright to be the trusted change-up; SI (July 2026) notes staff "confidence remains low" in the backups after inconsistent 2025 tape (Gordon: 199 yards, 2.8 avg, 3 TD).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Size/power skillset the staff wants to develop; must prove he can be relied on if Achane is hurt.
4. **Competition:** Head-to-head with Jaylen Wright; also Donovan Edwards, Anthony Hankerson, Carlos Washington Jr. pushing for depth roles (new regime with "no roster ties" per SI).
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Goal-line/short-yardage vulture plus insurance touches.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** the Achane handcuff race is the story — if Gordon wins clear No. 2, he's a priority handcuff given Achane's injury history. **Dynasty:** speculative young back worth a late stash.

### Jaylen Wright — RB (change-of-pace, in a battle)
1. **Role:** Competing for the No. 2/3 role and explosive-play snaps.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Battling Gordon; 2025 was uneven (288 yards, 4.1 avg, 2 TD in 10 games).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Speed profile complements Achane in theory; staff wants consistency.
4. **Competition:** Gordon II primarily; the new regime is genuinely evaluating the whole room.
5. **Injury:** None reported.
6. **Projected usage:** Rotational; usage spikes only on an Achane absence.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** deep-league handcuff dart. **Dynasty:** low-cost upside based on athletic traits.

*(Depth: Donovan Edwards, Anthony Hankerson, Carlos Washington Jr. competing for jobs; FB D.J. Herman replacing departed Alec Ingold — no fantasy relevance. Le'Veon Moss signed then retired.)*

## New York Jets

Year two of Aaron Glenn; Geno Smith returns to New York at QB. Breece Hall is paid but the staff preaches a committee; the WR room was rebuilt around Garrett Wilson, Adonai Mitchell, and first-rounder Omar Cooper Jr.

### Breece Hall — RB1 (paid, but "by-committee")
1. **Role:** Lead back after a fresh extension (~$43.5M), but the staff is explicitly committee-minded.
2. **Camp/preseason:** HC Aaron Glenn: "Breece is a top back; he's going to do outstanding. Then you have BA [Braelon Allen], then you have Isaiah [Davis]... I think we have a chance to run the ball really well." Hall (July 30, 2026, FOX Sports Radio NJ) said the Jets' "complete offense will help him maximize potential."
3. **Internal thoughts:** The organization wants Hall to graduate from big-play reliance to consistent, elite every-down production "worthy of top-tier compensation" (SI, 2026).
4. **Competition:** Braelon Allen (power) and Isaiah Davis (currently injured) for complementary touches — the committee messaging is the fantasy risk.

## Dallas Cowboys

6. **Usage/outlook:** 150+ target ceiling, 1,300+ yards and 8-10 TDs if healthy. Volume is bankable.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** Redraft — high-end WR1, top-8 overall pick. Dynasty — still a cornerstone but the down 2025 and Pickens' emergence slightly cap the ceiling; hold, don't panic-sell.

### George Pickens — WR2 (breakout on franchise tag)

6. **Usage/outlook:** 120+ targets, ~1,200-1,400 yards, high aDOT deep threat with real TD upside opposite Lamb.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** Redraft — mid-range WR1 / elite WR2, top-15 WR. Dynasty — the franchise tag adds contract uncertainty (could be tagged-and-traded or extended), but the on-field profile is ascending; a strong buy if a manager fears the tag situation.

## Los Angeles Rams

Continuity on offense: Stafford back under center for McVay, an elite WR duo in **Davante Adams** and **Puka Nacua**, and a lead back locked up long-term. The only real skill-position battle is WR3 (Tutu Atwell was released in free agency, opening a role).

### Kyren Williams (RB) — Locked-in starter, newly extended
1. **Role:** Unquestioned lead back. Signed a three-year, $33M extension (up to $38.35M, ~$11M/yr new money — 7th among RBs, between Aaron Jones and Josh Jacobs) through 2028 (LAFB Network, 2026).
2. **Camp/preseason:** Reported to camp amid the negotiation, then got paid; also took punt-return reps in camp (Turf Show Times, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** The extension cements him as the every-down back, though it "paints a murky picture" for how much is left for Corum.
4. **Competition:** Corum is the clear No. 2 and is pushing for a bigger slice (see below); Williams still leads.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Bell-cow volume, but expect Corum to eat into carries — Williams' ceiling is slightly lower than his past workhorse years.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** RB1/borderline RB1 — draft as a high-floor starter, but note the committee risk. **Dynasty:** signed through 2028; strong hold, though monitor the Corum split.

### Blake Corum (RB) — Ascending Year 3 back
1. **Role:** No. 2 back trending toward a near-even split. He logged 332 snaps (29%), 145 carries, 746 rushing yards and 5.1 YPC in 2025.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Arrived "a lot leaner" and more explosive after viral pre-camp workouts (NBC Sports, Aug 1, 2026); named an early camp "bright spot" (Aug 6, 2026). Williams himself confirmed Corum looks more explosive.
3. **Internal thoughts:** OC Nate Scheelhaase: "what you've seen from Blake is a guy that has a whole lot of confidence and what he's been able to build on from last year." Beat consensus: "A 50/50 split... is now realistic" (Yahoo, 2026).
4. **Competition:** Firmly the No. 2, well ahead of Hunter/Rivers; the only question is how much he takes from Williams.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Expanded early-down and change-of-pace role; standalone flex value with league-winning upside if Williams misses time.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** the best handcuff-with-standalone-value in the division — draftable as an RB3/flex, not just insurance. **Dynasty:** strong buy; ascending, cheap, and the McVay run scheme elevates backs.

### Jarquez Hunter (RB) — Year 2, make-or-break
1. **Role:** Competing for RB3. Was a healthy inactive with no offensive snaps for most of 2025.
2. **Camp/preseason:** "This feels like a big year for Hunter" (SI, 2026); needs to prove patience/vision issues are fixed and win a job on special teams.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Film reviews flagged running into blockers and missing cutback lanes; the talent (18.9% explosive-run rate at Auburn) is there, execution isn't yet.
4. **Competition:** Battling Ronnie Rivers and UDFA Dean Connors ("a dynamic playmaker") for the last RB spot.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Likely minimal offensively even if he makes the roster.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** none. **Dynasty:** deep-stash only — a Williams/Corum injury is his only path to relevance.

### Davante Adams (WR) — Elite vet
1. **Role:** The X-receiver and red-zone alpha; led the NFL in receiving TDs in 2025.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Providing "highlight moments" in early camp (therams.com Day 2, July 2026); mentoring rookie CJ Daniels.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Still the go-to scorer; his presence lets Nacua move around and takes coverage off everyone else.
4. **Competition:** Co-No. 1 with Nacua; no threat to his role.
5. **Injury:** Healthy, though age/injury history is the standing risk with the room's thin depth.
6. **Projected usage:** High-volume, TD-dependent WR1 numbers.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** back-end WR1/high WR2 — elite TD equity with Stafford. **Dynasty:** age-based sell-high candidate; win-now teams can still start him.

### Puka Nacua (WR) — Young superstar
1. **Role:** The offense's movable "chess piece" (Z, but plays everywhere) and arguably its best player.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Repeatedly the standout — "shines" on Day 9 (therams.com, 2026); anchored first-team offense in camp battles (Day 8).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Regarded internally as "one of the best wide receivers in the NFL" (SI depth-chart piece, July 24, 2026).
4. **Competition:** Untouchable co-No. 1.
5. **Injury:** Healthy; durability is the only historical caveat.
6. **Projected usage:** Massive target share; PPR monster.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** top-5 WR, first-round pick. **Dynasty:** blue-chip cornerstone; do not sell.

### CJ Daniels (WR) — Rookie, 6th round (WR3 sleeper)
1. **Role:** Front-runner-by-buzz for a rotational WR3 role behind Adams/Nacua; a bigger-framed contested-catch outside receiver from Miami.
2. **Camp/preseason:** "Turning heads" — caught nearly everything on Day 2 (July 28, 2026) and drew praise as a possible "keeper." Compared to a Josh Reynolds/DeMarcus Robinson type contributor.
3. **Internal thoughts:** McVay: "He's done a good job... no-nonsense mentality, but he enjoys football. And then he's really a great listener," and called him "a guy I'm looking forward to watching play." Davante Adams has publicly praised him.
4. **Competition:** Battling Jordan Whittington, Konata Mumpfield (Year 2) and slot/returner Xavier Smith for the WR3 job; the staff may rotate rather than name one.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Spot snaps and red-zone/contested looks early; upside if injuries hit the top two.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** deep-league sleeper/watch-list. **Dynasty:** worth a late rookie-pick stash — landing spot (Stafford, thin depth) is favorable.

### Rams WR3 committee — quick notes
- **Jordan Whittington** — physical, versatile, ball-carrying skills; the veteran incumbent option in the WR3 mix.
- **Konata Mumpfield** — Year 2 route-runner who "excels at getting open" but must improve at the catch point; a practice riser last year.
- **Xavier Smith** — slot speedster and return man; roster value tied to special teams.
*Verdict for all three:* redraft waiver-watch; dynasty deep-stash. Whoever wins the rotation has WR4/flex-in-bye upside given Stafford and the offense, but none is a confident draft pick.

## San Francisco 49ers

A dramatically new-look receiver room: **Deebo Samuel** was gone (traded in 2025), **Brandon Aiyuk** is off the active roster (reserve/left-squad list — GM John Lynch: "We wish him well"), and **Jauan Jennings** departed. In came **Mike Evans** (3yr/$42.5M) and **Christian Kirk** (1yr, ~$6M). The backfield still runs through a healthy Christian McCaffrey, with a wide-open RB2 race thinned by injuries.

### Christian McCaffrey (RB) — Elite vet, health the only question
1. **Role:** The workhorse RB1, entering his 10th season (age 30). Back and healthy to start camp.
2. **Camp/preseason:** A 49ers coach said "He looks really good this camp" (49ers Webzone, 2026). The team has publicly vowed to reduce his workload "even if he doesn't want it" (CBS SF, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** McCaffrey has waved off the age-30 RB narrative (SI, 2026); the org wants him fresher for January.
4. **Competition:** None for the lead role; the RB2 race is about keeping him fresh.
5. **Injury:** Healthy now (after an injury-shortened 2024).
6. **Projected usage:** Still a bell-cow in Shanahan's scheme, but planned load management could shave his ceiling and elevate the handcuff.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** first-round RB1 with a slight workload-management asterisk. **Dynasty:** win-now asset; age + workload plan make him a sell-high for rebuilders.

### Kaelon Black (RB) — Rookie, 3rd round (surging handcuff)
1. **Role:** Rookie competing for RB2 and, right now, the de facto top backup getting first crack behind McCaffrey. Indiana product (six college years; started at James Madison), 1,040 rush yards, 10 TD, 5.6 YPC on the 2025 CFP-champion Hoosiers.
2. **Camp/preseason:** With Jordan James (fractured ribs) and Isaac Guerendo (pec) both sidelined, Black has taken "significant reps" in August practices and is being evaluated extensively.
3. **Internal thoughts:** McCaffrey raved: "He's stacked, man... He's played mature football. I can't say enough good" (Niners Nation/Yahoo, July 2026). SF ranked him their second-best rusher prospect in the class.
4. **Competition:** Nominally battling Jordan James (who has a slight system-familiarity edge when healthy); injuries have handed Black the reps.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** In line to be the primary change-of-pace/insurance back; league-winning upside if McCaffrey misses time in this offense.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** the priority McCaffrey handcuff — draft him, not James. **Dynasty:** strong buy; talent + landing spot + a 30-year-old ahead of him.

### Jordan James (RB) — Year 2, RB2 hopeful (injured)
1. **Role:** Competing for RB2 with a slight edge in scheme familiarity — but currently out.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Sidelined with fractured ribs, ceding reps to Black. Coaches liked his second-half-of-2025 practice work; had just six garbage-time carries as a rookie.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Two-plus years younger than Black and trusted by staff, but the injury timing hurts.
4. **Competition:** Losing ground to Black while hurt.
5. **Injury:** Fractured ribs (Aug 2026).
6. **Projected usage:** TBD — behind Black at the moment.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** late-round flier/second handcuff. **Dynasty:** hold — still young, but Black has jumped him.

### Isaac Guerendo (RB) — Year 3, roster in jeopardy
1. **Role:** Former RB2 now fighting to make the team.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Suffered a pectoral tear in May 2026; was expected to seek clearance by the end of camp.
3. **Internal thoughts:** "Faces an uphill battle" per the RB2-battle reporting; prior injuries and the Black/James emergence have diminished his standing.
4. **Competition:** Behind both Black and James.
5. **Injury:** Pec tear (May 2026); recovery/clearance pending.
6. **Projected usage:** Minimal if he sticks.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** none. **Dynasty:** sell/drop in most formats.

### Mike Evans (WR) — New team (signed 2026 FA from Tampa Bay)
1. **Role:** The new WR1, a six-time Pro Bowler; SF built the room around Evans, Kirk and Pearsall with Aiyuk off the roster.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Named the 49ers' "most intriguing" newcomer to watch (Heavy, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** Lynch: "Mike and Christian, we're excited about. I think to have two grown men... is really exciting."
4. **Competition:** Clear No. 1 target alongside TE George Kittle.
5. **Injury:** Healthy (age is the standing risk).
6. **Projected usage:** Boundary/red-zone alpha with Purdy — high TD equity.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** WR2 with WR1 TD upside in a strong offense — a fresh-team situation worth targeting. **Dynasty:** age-based sell-high; win-now teams can roster him on the 1-year-fits-all logic of his short guarantee.

### Ricky Pearsall (WR) — Year 3, breakout candidate
1. **Role:** The WR2 and a popular 2026 breakout pick.
2. **Camp/preseason:** "Gearing up to silence doubters" (49ers Webzone, 2026); NFL.com's Nick Shook predicted a breakout, reasoning "Pearsall will operate in a deeper receiver room that won't allow opposing defenses to key on him."

### Christian Kirk (WR) — New team (signed 2026 FA, 1yr ~$6M)

### 49ers depth WRs — quick notes

## Seattle Seahawks

Defending Super Bowl LX champions, but with a reworked backfield: Super Bowl MVP **Kenneth Walker III** left for Kansas City (3yr/$45M) in March 2026, and **Zach Charbonnet** tore his ACL in the postseason. Seattle drafted **Jadarian Price** in the first round to headline a committee. The passing game is elite around **Jaxon Smith-Njigba**, now the NFL's highest-paid receiver.

### Jadarian Price (RB) — Rookie, 1st round (No. 32), Notre Dame
1. **Role:** Co-lead of a thinned backfield — the first Seahawks first-round RB since Rashaad Penny (2018) and just the fourth in franchise history. Drafted as Walker's replacement.
2. **Camp/preseason:** ESPN's Brady Henderson (Aug 5, 2026): "Early indications suggest that Holani and Price will be the top two options, with Wilson mixing in." Price "offers rushing versatility and receiving capability."
3. **Internal thoughts:** Seen as the future lead back; with Charbonnet out, he's "expected to receive significant early-season carries."
4. **Competition:** In a genuine timeshare with incumbent George Holani; power-back Emanuel Wilson complements. Charbonnet looms once healthy.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Committee back with three-down upside; volume could climb quickly if he separates from Holani.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** upside RB2/flex — draft capital + vacated Walker touches make him a target, but temper for the committee. **Dynasty:** cornerstone buy; the clear long-term lead once Charbonnet's future is sorted.

### George Holani (RB) — Incumbent, camp riser
1. **Role:** Currently co-atop the depth chart with Price as the No. 1/No. 2 options.
2. **Camp/preseason:** "Impressed as both a runner and pass-catcher, rising in coaches' estimations" through the offseason (ESPN's Henderson, Aug 5, 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** The staff trusts him more than his profile suggests; he's genuinely pushing the rookie for early snaps.
4. **Competition:** Splitting the lead role with Price; ahead of Emanuel Wilson.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Meaningful early-down and passing-down work while Charbonnet recovers — real standalone value in the near term.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** sneaky late-round flex with early-season volume; a cheaper way into this backfield than Price. **Dynasty:** hold/speculative — value tied to the pre-Charbonnet window.

### Zach Charbonnet (RB) — Injured (ACL), stash
1. **Role:** The 2025 co-starter (rushed for 12 TDs) is sidelined for much of 2026.
2. **Camp/preseason:** On the shelf; "the timeline for his availability remains unclear" (Aug 5, 2026). Surgery came after the Super Bowl (ACL tear, Jan/Feb 2026).
3. **Internal thoughts:** When healthy, he rejoins "a three-player committee."
4. **Competition:** Price and Holani are handling his workload in the interim.
5. **Injury:** ACL tear (postseason 2025 / surgery early 2026); out for a significant chunk of 2026.
6. **Projected usage:** Uncertain — depends on his return date and how entrenched Price is by then.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** injured-reserve stash / late flier only. **Dynasty:** buy-low window while he's hurt, but the Price pick clouds his path.

### Emanuel Wilson (RB) — New team (2026 FA), power complement
1. **Role:** The "powerful complement" mixing in behind Price/Holani; noted pass-catching ability.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Signed to add short-yardage/power thump to the committee.
3. **Competition:** Third in the pecking order; also fending off Kenny McIntosh, Cam Akers, Velus Jones Jr. and Jacardia Wright for depth spots.
4. **Injury:** Healthy.
5. **Projected usage:** Situational/goal-line.
6. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** waiver-only. **Dynasty:** negligible.

### Jaxon Smith-Njigba (WR) — Superstar, coming off a historic year
1. **Role:** Bona fide WR1 and offensive centerpiece. Signed an extension making him the league's highest-paid receiver.
2. **Camp/preseason:** All-business "run it forward" mindset (July 26, 2026 presser): "It's always attacking, getting bigger, faster, stronger. That second year of growth with Sam and the team and playing off 'Shid and Coop, just evolving in that way."
3. **Internal thoughts:** Coming off an all-time season and viewed as a top-tier NFL receiver.
4. **Competition:** Untouchable No. 1; Kupp/Shaheed play off him.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Elite target volume. 2025 line: **1,793 yards (8th-most in NFL history), a franchise-record 119 catches on just 163 targets, First-team All-Pro, and AP Offensive Player of the Year** — led the NFL in receiving yards.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** top-3 WR, first-round pick. **Dynasty:** premier cornerstone; do not sell.

### Cooper Kupp (WR) — Veteran WR2
1. **Role:** The steady No. 2, entering his 9th season; led Seattle in receiving in both the divisional round and the Super Bowl.
2. **Camp/preseason:** Established chemistry with Darnold; mentoring Tory Horton.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Reliable possession/red-zone target; JSN is clearly WR1 and "it's not close" per SI framing.
4. **Competition:** Ahead of Shaheed/Horton/MVS for the No. 2 role, but target competition is real.
5. **Injury:** Healthy (durability the recurring caveat).
6. **Projected usage:** 2025 line was modest (47-593 on 70 targets); a complementary volume in a JSN-dominant offense.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** WR4/flex with a safe-ish floor and playoff-tested role. **Dynasty:** age-based sell/hold.

### Rashid Shaheed (WR) — Ascending deep threat
1. **Role:** The vertical/field-stretcher, acquired via trade mid-2025 (15-188 in nine games plus three return TDs). Coaches "expect significantly increased offensive involvement after his first full offseason."
2. **Camp/preseason:** Part of JSN's "'Shid and Coop" trio; poised for a bigger role.
3. **Internal thoughts:** Viewed as a speed element to complement JSN's underneath dominance.
4. **Competition:** Battling Kupp/Horton for the No. 2/No. 3 target share.
5. **Injury:** Healthy.
6. **Projected usage:** Big-play/return value; boom-bust weekly line.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** deep-league flex dart with spike weeks. **Dynasty:** speculative hold; role could grow.

### Tory Horton (WR) — Year 2, back to full health (breakout buzz)
1. **Role:** A young WR climbing the WR3/4 mix; "might create a good problem for Mike Macdonald" (12th Man Rising, 2026).
2. **Camp/preseason:** "Turning heads by making 'multiple plays every day'" (NFL.com, 2026) and scored twice in a camp session (Heavy, 2026). Macdonald is managing his snaps on return — sat one practice, limited reps, but participated in pads and caught a 20-yard over-the-middle ball Friday (Spokesman-Review, Aug 1, 2026).

6. **Projected usage:** Ramp-up early, with real target/return upside as he gets back to full speed.
7. **Fantasy verdict:** **Redraft:** late-round upside stash/watch-list — monitor camp ramp. **Dynasty:** buy-low on a talented Year 2 returning from injury; the "unicorn" upside is worth a rookie-pick-level price.
