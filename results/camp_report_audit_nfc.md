# Fact-Check Audit — `2026_NFL_RB_WR_Camp_Report.md`, NFC sections only

**Auditor:** player-context-researcher
**Audit date:** 2026-08-09
**Scope:** NFC East (line 1452) through end of document (NFC West, line 2822). AFC sections not audited.
**Method:** Every high-stakes claim checked against current (2026) reporting — league news, Wikipedia
2026-season pages (roster/transaction tables dated Aug 2–8, 2026), and ESPN team depth charts pulled
Aug 9, 2026. Claims are labeled CONFIRMED / **ERROR** / DISPUTED / UNVERIFIED.

**Note on search budget:** the session's web-search quota was exhausted partway through. Later
verification was done via direct page fetches (Wikipedia 2026-season transaction tables, ESPN depth
charts, team sites). Coverage is complete for priorities 1–3 and most of 4; a handful of contract
figures and blog-sourced camp-buzz claims could not be independently sourced and are listed under
"Unverifiable" rather than guessed at.

## 1. CONFIRMED ERRORS — ordered by fantasy consequence

### E1. Ricky Pearsall (SF) is on Injured Reserve. The report calls him healthy and a breakout WR2.
- **Report says** (line 2653–2660): "Ricky Pearsall (WR) — Year 3, breakout candidate… **Locked in as No. 2
  behind Evans**… **Healthy entering camp**… Redraft: high-upside WR4/flex sleeper — cheap cost, real path."
- **Actually:** Pearsall is on **Injured Reserve**. ESPN's 49ers depth chart (retrieved Aug 9, 2026)
  lists him with an **IR** designation. The 2026 49ers season page (roster table dated Aug 8, 2026)
  likewise carries him as Injured Reserve. Reporting around the Deebo Samuel signing states San
  Francisco moved "one day after disclosing presumptive starting wide receiver Ricky Pearsall might be
  out for a while" (NBC Sports Bay Area, 2026).
- **Sources:** ESPN 49ers depth chart (accessed Aug 9, 2026); Wikipedia *2026 San Francisco 49ers
  season*, roster/reserve table dated Aug 8, 2026; NBC Sports Bay Area, "Why Deebo Samuel's surprising
  return to receiver-needy 49ers… makes sense" (2026).
- **Impact:** The report tells the owner to draft Pearsall as a cheap breakout. He is unrosterable in
  redraft as written. **Highest-consequence error in the NFC sections.**

### E2. Deebo Samuel is on the 49ers roster. The NFC West section says he is gone.
- **Report says** (line 2606, 49ers intro): "**Deebo Samuel** was gone (traded in 2025)" — he appears
  nowhere in the 49ers WR dossiers or depth notes.
- **Actually:** Samuel **re-signed with San Francisco on a 1-year deal worth up to $7M** and is on the
  active roster. The 2026 49ers transaction table dates the signing **August 1, 2026** (i.e. during
  camp, after most of the report's other 49ers sourcing). ESPN's depth chart lists "Deebo Samuel Sr."
  among the 49ers WRs.
- **Sources:** NBC Sports Bay Area, "Deebo Samuel reportedly returning to 49ers on one-year contract
  worth up to $7M" (2026); Wikipedia *2026 San Francisco 49ers season*, transactions (Aug 1, 2026);
  ESPN 49ers depth chart (accessed Aug 9, 2026).
- **Note — internal contradiction:** the report's own NFC East preamble (line 1461) and its accuracy
  caveat (line 20) both state correctly that "Deebo Samuel did NOT return [to Washington] — he
  re-signed with the 49ers." The NFC West section contradicts the rest of the document. This is a
  stale-source failure of exactly the Rashee Rice type: the 49ers section was built on pre-August
  sourcing (49ers Webzone March 29, 2026; ESPN late-July camp intel) that predates the signing.
- **Impact:** Materially changes the SF target hierarchy the report projects for Evans, Kirk and the
  (already-IR) Pearsall.

### E3. Kyler Murray is not on the Cardinals. He was released and signed with Minnesota.
- **Report says** (line 2446, NFC West QB context): "**Kyler Murray is working back from a foot injury
  with a murky timeline.**"
- **Actually:** Arizona **released Murray on March 11, 2026**, ending seven seasons. He signed a
  **one-year deal with the Minnesota Vikings** shortly after (Arizona retains most of his $36.8M 2026
  salary; Minnesota pays ~$1.3M).
- **Sources:** Wikipedia *2026 Arizona Cardinals season* ("was released on March 11"); AOL/AP,
  "Minnesota Vikings sign Kyler Murray to 1-year contract"; Yahoo Sports, "Kyler Murray's 2026 status
  with Cardinals after Mike LaFleur hiring."
- **Impact:** Removes the report's stated downside risk to Brissett's job, and — see E4 — the report
  misses where Murray actually went.

### E4. Vikings QB: the report omits Kyler Murray entirely and names McCarthy the presumed starter. Murray is the frontrunner.
- **Report says** (line 2011): "QB **J.J. McCarthy** is the presumed starter but there was an open **QB
  competition** in camp." Murray's name never appears. Line 2082 lists the Vikings QB as an
  unresolved item without mentioning who McCarthy is competing against.
- **Actually:** Minnesota's QB room is **Kyler Murray, J.J. McCarthy, Carson Wentz, Max Brosmer**.
  Murray has taken roughly **two-thirds of first-team 11-on-11 snaps** and is widely reported as the
  heavy favorite; multiple outlets have him as QB1 on the depth chart ahead of McCarthy.
- **Sources:** Bleacher Report, "Carson Wentz Signs Vikings Contract, Updated Depth Chart with Kyler
  Murray, J.J. McCarthy" (2026); Heavy, "Vikings Insider Issues Definitive Update on Kyler Murray,
  J.J. McCarthy Battle"; Vikings.com, "Vikings QBs J.J. McCarthy & Kyler Murray Aim to Maximize Every
  Camp Rep"; Star Tribune camp live blogs (Aug 4, 2026); Wikipedia *2026 Minnesota Vikings season*
  roster (Aug 2, 2026).
- **Impact:** The report applies a "QB-risk discount" to Justin Jefferson and caps Addison/Jennings
  ceilings on the premise of an unproven McCarthy. The likely starter is a two-time Pro Bowl veteran.
  This is a systematic mispricing of an entire pass game, not a detail.

### E5. Chris Brazzell II (CAR) is on Injured Reserve; the report presents him as an active WR3 challenger.
- **Report says** (lines 2243–2253): Legette "is projected as the third starting wide receiver, but

- **Sources:** Wikipedia *2026 Carolina Panthers season*, reserve list dated Aug 8, 2026; ESPN Panthers

- **Impact:** Cuts both ways — it invalidates Brazzell as a stash, and it removes the stated threat to
  Legette. Legette's WR3 role is considerably safer than the report says.
- **Provenance flag:** the Brazzell claim traces to a single **July 16, 2026** SI/OnSI depth-chart

## 2. DISPUTED — the report's claim conflicts with a better or more current source

### D1. Atlanta QB: the report frames Tua vs. Penix as a live competition. It is effectively decided.
- **Report says** (line 2139): Cousins released, Tua signed to compete with Penix; "**Penix was limited
  to open camp while finishing an injury rehab but 'expects to be full go'**… **the biggest swing
  factor for every Falcons pass-catcher.**"
- **Actually:** Penix is recovering from **surgery for a partially torn ACL suffered in Week 11 of
  2025** and **has not been fully medically cleared**. Current reporting describes the competition as
  "a battle in name only," with **Ian Rapoport reporting Tua will be the Week 1 starter** (opener at
  Pittsburgh, Sept 13). Penix himself: "I can't worry about a starting job until I'm worried about
  getting back on the field."
- **Sources:** SI/OnSI, "Tua Tagovailoa Opens Up About Falcons QB Competition With Michael Penix";
  Yardbarker (Aug 2026) x2; Last Word on Sports, "NFL Rumors: Analyst Reveals Falcons QB Winner"
  (Aug 7, 2026).
- **Note:** the underlying facts the report asserts — **Cousins released, Tua signed on a 1-year deal,
  Stefanski is HC** — are all **CONFIRMED**. The error is in characterizing the injury (a rehab of
  unspecified type vs. a post-op ACL with no clearance) and therefore in framing the QB outcome as
  genuinely uncertain. For Drake London's projection this reduces, not increases, QB volatility.

### D2. Arizona WR1: the report says Michael Wilson has passed Marvin Harrison Jr. The depth chart says otherwise.
- **Report says** (lines 2493–2509): Wilson is "**The Cardinals' WR1**… **the clear No. 1 over Harrison
  and Bourne**"; Harrison is the "nominal WR2… trending down."
- **Actually:** ESPN's Cardinals depth chart (accessed **Aug 9, 2026**) lists **Marvin Harrison Jr. as
  the starting WR**, with Michael Wilson well down the ordering. The report's basis is Pro Football
  Network aggregation of camp/social reaction and a quote from a content creator ("Zirksee") — camp
  buzz, not an official or reported depth-chart change.
- **Sources:** ESPN Cardinals depth chart (Aug 9, 2026) vs. Pro Football Network, "NFL world reacts to
  Marvin Harrison Jr. struggles" (late July / early Aug 2026).
- **Verdict:** Treat the Wilson-over-Harrison ordering as **speculation the report presents as fact**.
  The report's own fact/speculation separation failed here.

### D3. Giants WR2: Malachi Fields as the trending No. 2 is single-sourced blog speculation.
- **Report says** (lines 1538–1545): Fields "has made an early claim for the No. 2 receiver spot,"
  "leapfrogging Darius Slayton, Jalin Hyatt and JuJu Smith-Schuster," with Slayton "fading."
- **Actually:** ESPN's Giants depth chart (Aug 9, 2026) lists Fields **6th** among WRs — behind Nabers,
  Calvin Austin III, Odell Beckham Jr., Jalin Hyatt and Darius Slayton. Fields' draft slot (**3rd
  round, No. 74, Giants**) is CONFIRMED; the role claim is not.
- **Sources:** ESPN Giants depth chart (Aug 9, 2026) vs. GMENHQ, Aug 8, 2026 (the report's sole source
  for the entire Giants WR hierarchy — Fields' rise, Slayton's fade, JuJu "on thin ice," Hyatt's
  "nearly impossible roster odds" all trace to this one blog post).
- **Verdict:** Not an error, but the Giants WR room is the thinnest-sourced section in the NFC and
  should not be drafted off.

### D4. Tyler Allgeier: the report says both "traded to Arizona" and "signed in free agency."
- **Report says:** NFC South (line 2163) — "Tyler Allgeier **was traded** to Arizona this offseason."
  NFC West (lines 2455, 2466) — "**signed Tyler Allgeier in free agency**," "New team (signed 2026 FA
  from Atlanta)."
- **Actually:** The **fact that Allgeier is a 2026 Cardinal is CONFIRMED** (he appears on the Cardinals'
  2026 roster and is RB2 on ESPN's Aug 9 depth chart). The **mechanism (trade vs. free agency) could
  not be resolved** from available sources.
- **Impact:** None for fantasy. Flagged because the document contradicts itself.

### D5. Wan'Dale Robinson's Titans deal: 4 years or 3 years?
- **Report says** (line 1459): "Wan'Dale Robinson left in free agency (Titans, **4yr/$78M**)."
- **Actually:** Wikipedia's *2026 New York Giants season* transaction table lists **3 years, $78M**.
  The report's own cited NFL.com URL slug reads "four-year-78-million-deal." Sources conflict on
  years; **the $78M and the team are consistent across both**.
- **Impact:** None for NFC fantasy (he is a Titan either way).

## 6. Root-cause pattern

Every confirmed error in the NFC traces to one of two mechanisms:

1. **Sourcing that predates a July/August transaction.** E2 (Deebo signed Aug 1; the 49ers section
   leans on a March 29 Webzone piece and late-July ESPN camp intel), E1 (Pearsall's IR), E5 (Brazzell's
   IR, section built on a July 16 depth chart). This is structurally the same failure as the Rice
   error the owner caught — content that was true when written, presented as current.
2. **A whole-team premise never re-verified after a March move.** E3/E4 (Murray released Mar 11 and
   signed in Minnesota — the report misses him on *both* ends, leaving Arizona with a phantom injured
   starter and Minnesota with a phantom open competition) and E6 (Kubiak's departure).

**Recommended pre-draft rule:** for every NFC team, re-check the official depth chart and the
reserve/IR list within 48 hours of drafting. Six of the ten confirmed errors would have been caught by
that single step.
