**Method note / budget constraint (read this).** The session's web-*search* quota was exhausted
before this digest began, so all sourcing here is by **direct fetch** of structured pages that
carry explicit dates: ESPN team injury feeds (which timestamp each entry and attribute the
underlying beat reporter), ESPN team news rails, ESPN depth charts, and FantasyPros player
pages. That is enough for dated injury facts and is in fact *more* date-reliable than search
snippets. It is weaker for beat-writer color and practice-report narrative, and I flag every
place that bit. **Two known artifacts of these sources, both of which I have controlled for:**

1. **FantasyPros stamps its evergreen "expert note" with the page-load date.** Several players'
   pages returned "August 24, 2026" for what is plainly an older item (Bijan's extension is
   Aug 4; Bucky Irving's "full-go to begin training camp" is early August). I have **discarded
   every undated-but-Aug-24-stamped "expert note"** as a news item and used only entries with a
   genuine relative timestamp ("5 days ago") or an ESPN-dated injury row. This is exactly the
   recycled-news failure mode flagged in the brief, arriving through a different door.
2. **ESPN depth-chart pages parse badly through the fetcher** (the Seattle chart returned Cooper
   Kupp as a tight end; the Carolina WR chart returned what is clearly a full-roster dump).
   Depth-chart *ordering* is used below only where it is corroborated, and is marked ⚠️ where not.

**Prior-year contamination screen (explicit).** McCaffrey's **calf strain was 2024** and is NOT
reported here as current — see §SF. Charbonnet's ACL is the **2025 playoffs**, correctly aged.
McBride's extension is **April 2025** (already corrected last week, not repeated as new). No item
below is carried over from a prior season without a 2026-dated source.

**What this means for pick 1.05 — and what the "could be a month" line actually was.** The
Aug 16–17 picture that generated the alarm (a week without practice, a vague "tightness" label,
no timeline, a floated hold-in) has **fully deflated, and deflated in the benign direction**:
the absence was **scheduled load management by the player**, the contract theory was **denied on
the record by the head coach**, and he was back on the field with 2.5 weeks to the opener.
Shanahan's "could be a month" remark was a **conditional about playing through a nagging issue,
not a prognosis**, and nothing since has activated that branch.

**The residual risk on McCaffrey is not this injury — it is the base rate.** He is entering an
**age-30 season off 311 carries / 450 total touches including playoffs, at 3.9 YPC** (ESPN player
page, 2025 season line), and he **led all RBs in snaps, routes, touches, targets, receptions and
fantasy points** in 2025 — i.e. the RB1 finish was volume-built on a body that has broken down
before. Price the workload-and-age risk. **Do not price a phantom August injury**, and do not let
the 2024 calf strain leak into the 2026 file.
